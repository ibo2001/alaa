# Demo video script (English master)

**Length:** 1:55 (limit 2:00). **Format:** 1080×1920 vertical or 1920×1080 with the phone centred; burned-in captions in both languages. **Sound:** voice-over and natural sound only, no music (respects viewers who avoid it, and keeps the voice clear).

**Rules for this video**
- No verse text is typed in captions, titles or voice-over. Verses appear only as the app renders them on screen (from the verified source); the voice-over names references ("Al-Waqi'ah 68 to 70"), never recites or paraphrases.
- Every claim on screen is one we can show: numbers from `docs/RESULTS.md`, review status from `sources/REVIEW_LOG.md`.
- Use the live app (alaa-alpha.vercel.app), Arabic interface for the main flow, a few seconds in English.

## Captions
Burn in captions for every voice-over line (English master; Arabic version after approval). Keep each caption to two lines; never caption verse text.

## Before publishing
- Watch once with the sound off: the story must still be clear from captions and screens.
- Check every number against `docs/RESULTS.md` on the day of recording.
- Upload unlisted (YouTube or Drive), test the link from another device, and put it in the submission.
